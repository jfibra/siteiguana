# Module 6: Public Marketing Website & Landing Pages

## Complete Project Prompt

Create a comprehensive public marketing website for Site Iguana that effectively communicates the value proposition, showcases services, builds trust, and converts visitors into customers. This website serves as the primary marketing channel and should reflect the premium quality of Site Iguana's services.

## Project Overview

Site Iguana needs a professional, conversion-focused marketing website that attracts potential customers, clearly explains the service offerings, demonstrates expertise and quality, and guides visitors through the decision-making process to become paying customers. The website should be a showcase of Site Iguana's own web development capabilities.

## Core Features Required

### 1. Homepage & Brand Presentation
- **Hero Section**: Compelling headline, value proposition, and clear call-to-action
- **Service Overview**: Clear explanation of website development and maintenance services
- **Trust Indicators**: Customer testimonials, portfolio showcases, and credibility markers
- **Process Overview**: Simple explanation of how Site Iguana works
- **Social Proof**: Customer logos, testimonials, and success metrics
- **Primary Call-to-Action**: Clear path to get started with services

### 2. Service Pages & Offerings
- **Website Development Services**: Detailed explanation of website creation process
- **Maintenance & Support**: Ongoing website management and support services
- **Custom Solutions**: Specialized services and custom development options
- **Industry Solutions**: Tailored solutions for specific industries or business types
- **Service Comparisons**: How Site Iguana compares to competitors and alternatives
- **Pricing Information**: Transparent pricing with plan comparisons

### 3. Portfolio & Case Studies
- **Website Showcase**: Gallery of completed websites with descriptions
- **Case Studies**: Detailed success stories with metrics and results
- **Industry Examples**: Examples from different industries and business types
- **Before/After Comparisons**: Transformations and improvement demonstrations
- **Client Testimonials**: Video and written testimonials from satisfied customers
- **Process Documentation**: Behind-the-scenes look at development process

### 4. About & Trust Building
- **Company Story**: Site Iguana's mission, vision, and founding story
- **Team Profiles**: Meet the team behind Site Iguana's services
- **Company Values**: Core values and commitment to customer success
- **Quality Assurance**: Standards, processes, and quality guarantees
- **Certifications & Awards**: Industry recognition and professional certifications
- **Partnership Information**: Technology partners and professional associations

### 5. Resources & Content Marketing
- **Blog/Resource Center**: Helpful content about websites, business growth, and digital marketing
- **Guides & Tutorials**: Comprehensive guides for website planning and management
- **Industry Insights**: Market research, trends, and business insights
- **FAQ Section**: Comprehensive answers to common questions
- **Glossary**: Definitions of technical terms and industry jargon
- **Downloadable Resources**: Templates, checklists, and planning tools

### 6. Contact & Conversion
- **Contact Information**: Multiple ways to reach Site Iguana team
- **Contact Forms**: Lead capture forms with qualification questions
- **Live Chat Integration**: Real-time support and sales assistance
- **Consultation Booking**: Online booking for discovery calls and consultations
- **Quote Requests**: Detailed quote request forms with project information
- **Social Media Integration**: Links to and integration with social media profiles
